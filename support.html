Perihelion for iPhone

Need a hand, found a bug, or have an idea? Email lukicov-a5aagn@mail.grokbot.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and which version of Perihelion (it is shown at the bottom right of the title screen).

How do I play?
Tap anywhere on the title screen to begin. Your comet circles a star. Tap to let go and it flies off along the dotted line. Touch the ring of the next star and it catches you. Catch each star before it burns out, and leave on your first lap to build flow.
What are the hearts?
You start with three. A miss spends one and puts you back on the last star you caught. You earn a heart back every four stars, up to the number you started with.
Can I make it easier or harder?
Yes. Tap Adjust on the title or game-over screen, or on the pause screen, to change the difficulty, how far the comet flies, and how many lives you start with.
How do I pause?
Tap the two bars at the top right during play. The game also pauses by itself when you leave the app.
How do I turn the sound off?
Use the Sound button at the top right of the title and game-over screens. The game is also silent when your iPhone's ring/silent switch is on silent.
Does Perihelion collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
Is it on Android, or iPad?
Perihelion is for iPhone. There is a browser version of the original game that works anywhere.
My scores disappeared.
Scores live only on your device, so deleting the app, or erasing your phone, deletes them.
