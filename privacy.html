Privacy policy

Perihelion for iPhone · Last updated 26 November 2026

Perihelion is made by Gleb Lukicov.

We collect no data

Perihelion has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party software development kits and no network connection. Nothing you do in the app is sent off your device, to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

So the game can remember you, it saves your best score, your furthest star, whether sound is on, and your difficulty, comet reach and lives settings. This is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version adds something that does collect data, such as a Game Center leaderboard, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
