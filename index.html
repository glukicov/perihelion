Tap to let go of your star. Catch the next one before this one burns out.

The quiet parts

Just the game

  • Offline

    It plays with no connection, on your phone or in the browser.

  • No ads

    Nothing is sold over the chart, and nothing breaks into a run.

  • No tracking

    No analytics and no third-party kits. Nothing you do in the game is sent off your device.

  • No account

    There is no sign-in. Scores stay on your device, and deleting the app deletes them.
